ABOUT US

Practical Learning for Real-World QA

Genix Solutions is focused on helping learners connect software testing knowledge with the practical work of a QA professional.

OUR APPROACH

Built around real project work.

Our programme is designed around realistic scenarios, a continuous reference project and the workflows that connect requirements, testing, defects, automation and release decisions.

The goal is not simply to teach students how to answer testing questions. It is to help them understand what to do when they join a real QA team.

TWO BUSINESS AREAS

Services and training, together

Genix Solutions delivers offshore software testing services to businesses and develops practical QA skills through an industry-focused training programme. Our testing experience informs the practical scenarios in training.

TEACHING PRINCIPLES

How we teach

No concept without context

Every idea is introduced through a situation learners could meet on a real team.

No tool without a reason

Tools are taught when the work calls for them, not in isolation.

No exercise without a realistic scenario

Practice mirrors real project work.

Interview confidence from understanding

Interview readiness comes from practical understanding, not memorisation.
