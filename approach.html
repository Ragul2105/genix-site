OUR APPROACH

Learn by Doing, Not Just Watching.

Each major topic starts with a practical situation and gives learners the opportunity to think, investigate, perform, discuss and reflect.

THE LEARNING CYCLE

Eight steps in every chapter.

1. Real World

Meet a realistic QA situation.

2. Think

Decide what you would do.

3. Learn

Introduce the relevant concept.

4. See

Watch professional practice demonstrated.

5. Do

Perform the activity yourself.

6. Discuss

Review decisions, mistakes and alternatives.

7. Interview

Turn the experience into an interview answer.

8. Reflect

Consider how you would handle it on the job.

THE CONTINUOUS PROJECT

Follow one application. Understand the entire QA process.

When learners study APIs, they investigate what happens behind the cart. With databases, they validate order information. With automation, they automate regression scenarios. With CI/CD, those tests become part of a pipeline.

Search → Product → Cart → Login → Address → Delivery → Coupon → Payment → Order → Notification → My Orders

Browser/UI → API → Application Services → Database → External Systems

BEYOND E-COMMERCE

The same principles apply across industries.

Examples from banking, travel and SaaS show how QA principles transfer between domains.

Client applications or client-derived materials are used for training only where the necessary permissions, confidentiality protections and data safeguards are in place.
