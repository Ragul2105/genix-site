QA PROGRAMME

Industry-Ready QA & Software Testing Programme

Learn the theory. Experience the work. Build confidence. Become job-ready.

OVERVIEW

Bridge the gap between training and real QA work.

The programme connects testing concepts, tools and exercises to a continuous production-style project, so learners see how skills fit together in a commercial software team.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Traditional: concept, tool, exercise, interview preparation.

Genix: real scenario, think, learn, see professional practice, perform, discuss, interview.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

Three levels of confidence.

Knowledge confidence

Understand testing concepts, terminology, tools and professional practices.

Interview confidence

Answer practical and scenario-based questions because you have worked through similar situations.

Job confidence

Understand the workflow of a real QA project and contribute sooner after joining a company.

The programme gives realistic project exposure to make the transition into a QA role smoother. It does not claim that completing a course makes you an experienced employee.
